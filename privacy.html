Mantis Updater - Privacy

Mantis Updater is published by Mantis Networks (Pty) Ltd. It works on the Mantis calculator you have open in Excel.

What stays on your computer

The patch table, settings and log are stored by the add-in in Excel on your computer. Changes made by patches are saved in your calculator.

What is sent when you use AI

Only your question, the AI Guide text and the scorecard values are sent. Formulas, input sheets and client documents are never sent.

  • Mantis AI: the request goes to the Mantis AI service, together with the calculator's product, version and registration reference so that your AI Tier can be checked. Mantis records a question category and a count, not your question text.
  • My own key: the request goes directly from Excel to Anthropic or OpenAI using the key you entered in your calculator. The key is stored only in your calculator file and is never sent to Mantis.

Patch downloads

When you click Fetch from Mantis, the add-in downloads the patch table from the address in Settings. Nothing about you or your calculator is sent.
