Mantis Updater - Support

Mantis Updater applies Mantis patches and AI tools to Mantis B-BBEE calculators that are already in use.

Using it

  1. Open your Mantis calculator in Excel.
  2. On the Home tab, click Mantis Updater.
  3. On the Update tab, click Apply updates to this calculator.
  4. On the AI tab, ask a question about your scorecard, or export a values-only snapshot.

Help

Email mike@mantisnetworks.co and include the text from the add-in's Log tab (Copy log).
